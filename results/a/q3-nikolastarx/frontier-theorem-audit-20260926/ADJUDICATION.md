# R9 frontier claim: source-level adjudication

The earlier [limited Step 2 audit](../frontier-semantic-audit-20260926/REPORT.md) correctly rejected the inference “221,208 B means official illegality.” Its uncertainty about whether the *original-compute* frontier is even a no-spill lower bound came from not inspecting the P3 Task builder. The [expanded proof](REPORT.md) inspected that builder. I independently checked the frozen `multicore_cut_evaluate_problem_3.py`, `schedule_step2.py`, `attention_rows.py`, and `layered_query_flow.py` in this worktree.

For a **valid, singleton R9 plan that reaches Step 2**, the Task builder retains every original tensor ID, size, local pool (`DDR` inputs become `UB`), and incidence with its local eligible compute ops. Priority sorting fixes the projection of Step 2's input sequence to the submitted compute word. Step 2 allocates a tensor at first use and frees after last use, so the original-tensor closed-interval frontier is a necessary lower bound on the hypothetical zero-spill peak. R9's `_ports` rejects direct op-to-op edges; therefore no extra local tensor arises from direct-edge conversion. Generated COPYs use original tensors in the bucket of their earliest local consumer or latest local producer, so within that guarded class the frontier is the exact hypothetical no-spill Task peak. These statements are about the *source-defined conditional model*, not a recorded official Task run.

The recorded 068/K5 static UB peak of **221,208 B** exceeds the configured **131,072 B**. Thus this exact R9 mapping and word cannot pass Step 2 with zero spills if it reaches Step 2. Step 2 may spill and continue, so neither official plan legality nor P3 Makespan follows. A changed word or ownership requires a fresh frontier calculation. The diagnostic did not run Task, Step 2, Step 3, or E0; no benchmark number was produced here.

This is useful as a cheap rejection rule only when the intended design explicitly requires zero spill. It must not reject potentially useful spilled plans by claiming they are illegal. The next bounded semantic check, if separately budgeted, is to build the exact 068 Task/sequence and inspect Step 2 spill records, then evaluate whole-plan legality and P3/P2 separately.
